# Product benchmark: reviewing and uploading creative assets in a browser (KILN-35)

Date: 2026-11-07. Job: an artist or lead opens the studio's assets, finds one, uploads files or a new version, and inspects an asset's versions before reviewing it. The owner's verdict on Kiln v0.1 was "looks amateur"; this table records how leading review and asset tools solve the same job and what Kiln does as a result.

References (read on the date above; products change):

- **Frame.io V4** (Adobe, web): help centre "Uploading your media" (https://help.frame.io/en/articles/9101026-uploading-your-media) and the V4 launch notes "Easy asset findability" (https://blog.frame.io/2024/04/30/frame-io-v4-beta-feature-easy-asset-findability-workspaces/).
- **Eagle 4** (desktop asset library): "Interface – Overview" and "Interface – Toolbar" (https://en.eagle.cool/support/article/interface-toolbar).
- **Figma file browser** (web): help centre "Search for files, projects and people" and the community threads on sort and filter persistence (forum.figma.com, 2024–2025).
- **Dropbox Replay** (web): "How to compare video versions in Dropbox Replay" (https://help.dropbox.com/view-edit/dropbox-replay-compare-video-versions).

No account-only screens were captured; the evidence is the vendors' own documentation above and the before/after screenshots of Kiln in `screens/`.
