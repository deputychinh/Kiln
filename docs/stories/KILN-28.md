# KILN-28: Free a worker stuck on a hung job

- Release: v0.2 · Status: doing · Owner: Claude (deliver-loop)

## Story

As an **operator running Kiln workers**, I want a job that hangs (a stuck native call, a network read that never returns) to cost at most its time budget, so that one bad file cannot take a worker out of service until someone restarts it, and so that stopping a worker never waits forever.

Today (KILN-24/25) the heartbeat stops extending a hung job's lease after 30 minutes, so another worker can retry the job, but the worker running it stays blocked for good, and a stop request waits for that job without a bound.

INVEST: Independent ☑ (worker only) · Negotiable ☑ · Valuable ☑ · Estimable ☑ · Small ☑ · Testable ☑

## Non-functional

- Reliability: a hard exit loses nothing that matters — the database rolls back the dropped connection, the thumbnail is written in one PUT, the model sandbox child exits when its parent dies (KILN-5), and the job is retried through the existing attempts/bury path.
- Observability: one error line per overrun with job id, version id, attempt, runtime and budget; the supervisor logs a kill of a stuck child and every restart reason.
- Security: none new (no new input, no new privileges).

## Out of scope

- One child process per job (spawn cost on every job; the per-process watchdog bounds a hang the same way).
- Releasing the lease early on overrun (the process may be wedged; expiry is the safe signal).

## Design

- `kiln_worker/watchdog.py`: `JobWatchdog` starts one daemon thread per job (`Worker.run_once` wraps each job in `watchdog.watch(job)`); the thread ends the process with `exit(WORKER_OVERRAN_EXIT_CODE)` (`os._exit`) at the job's deadline (max runtime) or at stop + grace, unless the job finished at that moment. It also publishes the job and a sign of life every poll in the shared `JobBeacon`.
- Supervisor backstop (ADR 0009): native code that holds the GIL, or a frozen process, stops every thread of the child — the watchdog and the lease heartbeat included. The watchdog thread writes a sign of life to a lock-free shared `JobBeacon` (`RawArray`: job start, last sign of life, job id) every poll; the supervisor kills a child in a job that showed none for 60 s (`SUPERVISOR_LIVENESS_SECONDS`) or is still in one job 5 s past its runtime, then restarts at once.
- Deployment: the orchestrator's stop timeout must be at least grace + 10 s (70 s by default) — Kubernetes `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` (default 30 s), `docker stop --time` / compose `stop_grace_period` (default 10 s) — or the grace is cut short by SIGKILL (shutdown stays bounded either way).
- Constants: `WORKER_OVERRAN_EXIT_CODE = 76`, `JOB_STOP_GRACE_SECONDS = 60`, `SUPERVISOR_KILL_MARGIN_SECONDS = 10`, `SUPERVISOR_DEADLINE_MARGIN_SECONDS = 5`; setting `KILN_JOB_STOP_GRACE_SECONDS` (> 0).
- Supervisor: 76 restarts at once like 75 (with its own log line); on stop, `join(grace + margin)` then `kill()`.
- No API, data or UI change.
