Where the bundled password lists come from and under which terms (KILN-39, ADRs 0017 and 0019)

1. common-passwords.txt — the 100,000 most common passwords in Have I Been Pwned's Pwned
   Passwords, published by the UK National Cyber Security Centre in April 2019
   ("PwnedPasswordsTop100k.txt", "Passwords, passwords everywhere"). NCSC material is Crown
   copyright, reusable under the Open Government Licence v3.0
   (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), with attribution:
   "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0."
   Have I Been Pwned (https://haveibeenpwned.com, Troy Hunt) is the source of the underlying data.
   Its API terms say "there is no licencing or attribution requirements on the Pwned Passwords
   API" (https://haveibeenpwned.com/API/v3, read 2026-11-08; the CC BY 4.0 licence there covers the
   breach and paste APIs); the credit is given because it is welcome.
   Copy: unchanged from SecLists, Passwords/Common-Credentials/100k-most-used-passwords-NCSC.txt.
   SHA-256: c2e5696882c603b76bb67a47ee970897e5a76fc4c3f5547abe3d0ca340c576e0

2. long-breached-passwords.txt — every entry of 15 or more code points (after NFC), in the
   original order, of the top 1,000,000 (by frequency) of Mark Burnett's "Ten Million
   Passwords" (February 2015), a cleaned set of
   passwords from public breaches that he released to the public domain
   (https://medium.com/xato-security/today-i-am-releasing-ten-million-passwords-b6278bbe7495).
   Derived from SecLists, Passwords/Common-Credentials/xato-net-10-million-passwords-1000000.txt
   (SHA-256 424a3e03a17df0a2bc2b3ca749d81b04e79d59cb7aeec8876a5a3f308d0caf51): lines kept when
   len(unicodedata.normalize("NFC", line)) >= 15, nothing else changed; 10,908 lines.
   SHA-256 of the derived file: 977ebd3a68e793459a1f83c6766ccf75152f9268098d624d8dac7c28326d3c37
